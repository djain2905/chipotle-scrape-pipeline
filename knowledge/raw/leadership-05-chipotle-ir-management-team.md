# Management — Chipotle Mexican Grill

## Scott Boatwright — Chief Executive Officer

**Scott Boatwright** is the Chief Executive Officer and a member of the Board of Directors for Chipotle Mexican Grill. Appointed in November 2024, Mr. Boatwright previously served as Interim Chief Executive Officer since August 2024. Prior to that, he held the role of Chief Operating Officer/Chief Restaurant Officer since May 2017. Mr. Boatwright was named "Operations CREATOR of the Year" by Nation's Restaurant News in 2021 and named to Fast Casual's "2020 Top 25 Executives" list.

Prior to Chipotle, Mr. Boatwright spent 18 years with Arby's Restaurant Group in various leadership positions. He most recently served as the Senior Vice President of Operations, where he was responsible for the performance of over 1,700 Arby's restaurants in numerous states.

Mr. Boatwright is a member of Business Roundtable, Business Council, and National Restaurant Association's Fast Casual Industry Council. He holds a Master of Business Administration from the J. Mack Robinson College of Business at Georgia State University.

## Adam Rymer — Chief Financial Officer

**Adam Rymer** is the Chief Financial Officer for Chipotle Mexican Grill, responsible for corporate and field financial planning and analysis, as well as the financial reporting, tax, treasury and investor relations functions. He previously served as Vice President of Finance and has held a variety of positions in Finance and People Experience since joining the organization in 2009. In 2025, Mr. Rymer was named Extel All America Best CFO for the restaurant sector.

Prior to Chipotle, Mr. Rymer held corporate finance positions at Rock Bottom Restaurants and Travelocity and was also a Which Wich franchisee.

Mr. Rymer has a Bachelor of Business Administration degree in finance from the University of Texas at Austin.

---

## Curt Garner — President, Chief Strategy and Technology Officer

**Curt Garner** is the President, Chief Strategy and Technology Officer for Chipotle Mexican Grill. Prior to this, he served as the Chief Customer and Technology Officer for nearly a decade since joining the organization in November 2015. In 2020, Mr. Garner was named to the CIO Hall of Fame, and in 2025, he was featured on Forbes's CIO Next list.

Prior to Chipotle, Mr. Garner worked at Starbucks Corporation for 17 years, most recently serving as Executive Vice President and Chief Technology Officer.

Mr. Garner has a Bachelor of Arts degree in economics from The Ohio State University.

---

## Laurie Schalow — Chief Corporate Affairs and Food Safety Officer

**Laurie Schalow** is the Chief Corporate Affairs and Food Safety Officer for Chipotle Mexican Grill. In her role, Ms. Schalow oversees all internal and external communications, stakeholder engagement, sustainability, customer service, social listening, government affairs, food safety and quality assurance for the brand's 4,000+ restaurants. She is also President of The Chipotle Cultivate Foundation.

Prior to joining Chipotle in 2017, Ms. Schalow was Vice President of Public Affairs for Yum! Brands.

---

## Ilene Eskenazi — Chief Legal and Human Resources Officer

**Ilene Eskenazi** is the Chief Legal and Human Resources Officer for Chipotle Mexican Grill. Appointed in January 2026, Ms. Eskenazi previously served as the Chief Human Resources Officer for two years at the organization.

Prior to joining Chipotle, Ms. Eskenazi served as the Chief Legal and Human Resources Officer for Petco Health and Wellness Company. Before moving to Petco, Ms. Eskenazi was the Global General Counsel and Chief Human Resources Officer at Boardriders, Inc. (previously Quiksilver, Inc.).

Ms. Eskenazi serves on the Board of Directors of a.k.a. Brands Holding Corp. She holds a Juris Doctorate from UCLA School of Law and a Bachelor of Arts degree in philosophy from the University of Michigan.

## Jason Kidd — Chief Operating Officer

**Jason Kidd** is the Chief Operating Officer at Chipotle. He joined the organization in 2025 and is responsible for overseeing operations of the company's 4,000+ restaurants as well as development for its new restaurant openings.

Prior to Chipotle, Mr. Kidd served as the Global Chief Operating Officer at Taco Bell. He also held the role of President of Hearing Lab Technology, LLC and spent six years at 99 Cents Only Stores, most recently as President and COO. Additionally, he held roles at Sam's Club for 20 years.

Mr. Kidd holds a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from the Sam M. Walton College of Business at the University of Arkansas.

---

## Kerry Bridges — Chief Food Safety Officer

**Kerry Bridges** is the Chief Food Safety Officer for Chipotle Mexican Grill. Appointed in January 2026, Ms. Bridges previously served as Vice President of Food Safety.

Prior to joining Chipotle, Ms. Bridges oversaw supplier food safety for Wal-Mart. Her career also includes roles with TESCO and Jack in the Box, Inc.

She attended California Polytechnic State University, where she received a bachelor's degree in food science.

## Stephanie Perdue — Senior Vice President of Brand Marketing

**Stephanie Perdue** is the Senior Vice President of Brand Marketing, overseeing brand strategy, advertising, innovation, experiential, social media and brand activation. An expert marketer with over 20 years of experience in the restaurant industry.

Prior to Chipotle, Ms. Perdue served as the Chief Marketing Officer at TGI Fridays. She also held senior leadership roles at Taco Bell for over 12 years, ultimately serving as Chief Product Marketing Officer, responsible for driving marketing strategy and innovation, including leading the teams that designed Doritos Locos Tacos.

Stephanie holds an MBA from the UCLA Anderson School of Management and a business economics undergraduate degree from UCLA.

---

## Nate Lawton — Chief Business Development Officer

**Nate Lawton** is the Chief Business Development Officer for Chipotle Mexican Grill, overseeing international business development opportunities, emerging concepts and partnership strategy.

Prior to joining Chipotle, Mr. Lawton spent over 20 years at Procter & Gamble, ultimately serving as Vice President of North America Fabric Care. He also codeveloped and sold his own business, George Remus Bourbon, to MGP Ingredients Inc. in 2016.

He holds a bachelor's degree in business administration from Emory University's Goizueta Business School.

---

## Nevielle Panthaky — Senior Vice President of Culinary

**Nevielle Panthaky** is the Senior Vice President of Culinary at Chipotle Mexican Grill, overseeing culinary strategy, menu innovation, product commercialization and cross-functional collaboration. A classically trained chef who joined the organization in 2018.

He graduated from The Culinary Institute of America in 2003 and held culinary leadership roles at P.F. Chang's, Panda Restaurant Group and Dine Brands.

Mr. Panthaky holds an MBA from the University of California, Irvine's Paul Merage School of Business.

## Michael (MJ) Johnston — Vice President of Finance

**Michael Johnston** is the Vice President of Finance at Chipotle Mexican Grill, leading the company's global Finance organization. Mr. Johnston has spent nearly 11 years with Chipotle, progressing through roles of increasing responsibility within the Finance function.

Prior to Chipotle, Mr. Johnston served as a Financial Analyst at Accenture.

He holds a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration (Finance, Economics) from the University of Northern Colorado.

## Garrett Calderwood — Vice President of Operations Services

**Garrett Calderwood** is the Vice President of Operations Services at Chipotle Mexican Grill, overseeing strategies, tools and support that enable operational excellence across the company's restaurants. Mr. Calderwood joined Chipotle over 11 years ago, most recently serving as Regional Vice President responsible for over 450 restaurants across the Northeast U.S.

Before joining Chipotle, Mr. Calderwood was a Field Consultant for 7-Eleven.

He holds a Bachelor of Arts in History and Anthropology from Eckerd College.
